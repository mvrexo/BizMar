# BizMar – Business Management & Analytics Dashboard

**Urus Bijak, Bisnes Melonjak.**

BizMar is a web-based business management and analytics dashboard
developed using Python and Streamlit. It helps small business owners
manage daily business operations, monitor sales performance and
understand business trends through interactive visualizations.

## Key Features

- **Dashboard & Analytics** – Monitor sales, revenue, gross profit
  and business performance.
- **Order Management** – Create, update and track customer orders.
- **Customer Management** – Maintain customer records.
- **Product Management** – Manage product information, pricing and costs.
- **Payment Tracking** – Record payments and monitor outstanding balances.
- **Production Monitoring** – Track order deadlines and production status.
- **Business Intelligence** – Explore sales trends, product performance
  and customer insights.
- **Reports** – Generate business reports for further analysis.
- **User Interface** – Responsive interface with Light and Dark themes
  and English and Bahasa Melayu support.
